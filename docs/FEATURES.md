# Using Broom Home: a tour with the remote

Everything works with the arrow keys, **OK**, **Back** and **Home**. No mouse or keyboard needed.

## Home screen

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| Clock, greeting, date | Greeting changes with the time of day and alternates English / हिन्दी / ગુજરાતી. On festival days you get a festival greeting and decorations |
| Panchang line (under the date) | Gujarati date, tithi, current Choghadiya, and Rahu Kaal while it's on |
| Weather (top right) | Now, feels-like, humidity, wind, an air-quality warning, and a hint about the best time for a walk |
| Status line | Free RAM, free storage, Wi-Fi bars and latency |
| Ticker strip | Live India / IPL cricket score (its own line during a match), big-club football, then Nifty, Sensex, Bank Nifty, S&P, Nasdaq, $/₹ and gold. **OK** opens Briefing |
| Buttons | **Theme · Briefing · Panchang · Weather · Screensaver · Settings** |
| Favourites | Your apps in your order. **Long-press OK** on any app: Move left / right, Remove from / Add to Favourites |
| Recently used | Apps you opened most recently |
| Forecast strip | The next 8 hours and 7 days |
| Inputs | HDMI 1–3, AV and Live TV. Each HDMI tile shows the connected device (via HDMI-CEC), green when it's on and amber on standby. See [HDMI devices and power-on](#hdmi-devices-and-power-on-hdmi-cec) |
| App rows (scroll down) | Every installed app, grouped: Streaming, Music & Audio, Media & Files and so on |

**Home** on the remote always brings you back here, from any app or HDMI input.

## Settings (Quick settings)

The **Settings** button opens a list:
- TV settings: Picture & TV options, Sound, Display, Wi-Fi & network, Apps, All settings
- Broom screens: Themes, Weather, Screensaver, Briefing, Watchlist & market alerts, Network monitor, TV stats & savings
- **Broom sounds** on / off: the tick and click as you move around
- **Power-on** switch: *Broom (or HDMI device that's on)* opens Broom when the TV turns on, unless an HDMI device such as a Fire TV or set-top box is on, in which case it opens that input (see [HDMI devices and power-on](#hdmi-devices-and-power-on-hdmi-cec)). *Last input* goes straight back to whatever was on before
- **Live score pop-ups** on / off
- **Sleep timer**: 15 min to 2 h, with a 1-minute warning. Any key cancels it
- **Features: choose what to show**: tick boxes for Markets, Cricket scores, Football scores, Panchang, and each news category (Cricket, Football, Tech, AI, Space, Market news). Anything you untick is not downloaded and disappears from home, Briefing, the screensaver and pop-ups.

## Themes

**Theme** opens a gallery. Choose one of:
- **Auto**: the scene follows the time of day (dawn, day, dusk, night)
- a fixed scene: Kerala Backwaters, Desert Fort, Himalayan Peaks, Temple Lamps, Warli Art, Arctic Aurora, Alpine Lake, Canyon Sunset, Film Noir, Neon City, Retro Bollywood…

Festival decorations appear by themselves on the day: Diwali diyas, Navratri garba confetti, Uttarayan kites, Holi colours, Christmas snow, New Year fireworks, tiranga on 26 Jan / 15 Aug, and a toran on other festivals.

## Briefing

Opens from the button, from the ticker, or by itself on the first switch-on between 6 and 10 AM. Switch that last one off with the **Morning briefing** button on the screen.
- **Jump buttons** at the top: Summary · Indices · India sectors · India stocks · US tech & AI · Football · Cricket · News
- The page moves to the next tab every 2 minutes. Pressing any key pauses that for 2 minutes
- Every stock card shows price, % change and a 5-day mini chart. Each tracker shows its best and worst mover
- Market data is delayed about 15 minutes. It refreshes every 5 minutes while a market is open and hourly otherwise
- **Default lists:** India: large caps (Reliance, HDFC Bank, TCS, HAL, BEL…) plus the most-followed mid / small caps and new-age stocks (Eternal, Trent, Swiggy, Paytm, PB Fintech, Jio Financial, Dixon, Polycab, Persistent, Kaynes, BSE, CDSL, Mazagon Dock, Suzlon, IRFC, IndiGo). US tech & AI: Nvidia, Apple, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Tesla, Broadcom, AMD, TSMC and more. When a list is longer than 20, the Briefing screensaver page shows its 20 biggest movers
- **Watchlist**: add a stock (search by name or symbol), remove it, move it up or down, rename it, or reset to the defaults. New default stocks are added to an edited watchlist once, after an update. Set alerts for an index moving 1%+ or a stock moving 5%+. Alerts pop up over any app during market hours
- **Customise**: the same Features tick-list as in Settings
- **Refresh**: fetch everything now

## Weather

- Now, warnings and trends, sunrise and sunset
- **Walk / jog planner** for 3 days. It rates slots on temperature, humidity, rain, UV and air quality. When the air is bad it says "Poor all day: better to exercise indoors" instead of picking a "best" slot
- 24-hour and 14-day forecasts, and air quality (AQI, PM2.5)
- **Use TV location**: uses your Wi-Fi location, rounded to about 1 km. If no app has asked for the location yet (common on a debloated TV), Broom asks the TV for a fresh one. **Change area**: type an area, a city, or "lat, lon", then pick the right match from the list

Data comes from Open-Meteo (free, no account).

## Panchang

Vaar, tithi and nakshatra with end times, paksha, yoga, karana, Moon and Sun rashi, month (Gujarati amanta), Vikram and Shaka Samvat, and ritu. It also shows:
- sunrise and sunset for your area
- Abhijit, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda and Gulika
- day and night Choghadiya, with the current one highlighted
- festivals in the next 90 days

All of it is calculated on the TV, so it works offline.

## Screensaver

**Screensaver** button:
- **Style**: Nebula Drift, Aurora, Warp Speed, Rangoli Mandala, Minimal Clock, Facts & Quotes, Briefing, Panchang, photo packs (Monuments, Nature, Animals, Baby animals, Space), My photos (USB) or **Shuffle**. Shuffle changes style every 10 minutes and goes through all of them (the animations, Facts & Quotes and every photo pack) once each, in random order, before any repeats. **Preview** shows any style straight away
- **Start screensaver after**: how long with no remote activity
- **Turn TV off after**: a power saver while the screensaver is running
- **Briefing inside every screensaver**: every few minutes, swap to a market, score and news page, then back. Market pages only appear while a market is trading (Asia, India, Europe, or the US session in Indian evening hours, on weekdays). The rest of the time you see only scores and news
- **Night power saving**: from midnight to 6 AM, only the Minimal Clock, without the Briefing pages

Photo packs download featured pictures from Wikimedia, with a family-friendly filter, a credit on screen and a 50 MB cache. For **My photos (USB)**, plug in a stick with JPGs.

## HDMI devices and power-on (HDMI-CEC)

Broom uses HDMI-CEC, the standard that lets the TV and plugged-in devices talk over the HDMI cable, for two things:

- **Inputs row:** each HDMI tile shows the name of the device plugged in (for example "Amazon FireTV", a set-top box or a console), green when it's on and amber on standby. OK opens that input.
- **Power-on goes to the device that's on:** when the TV switches on, Broom waits a few seconds and checks the HDMI devices. If one is on, the TV opens its input instead of Broom. Broom checks several times in the first 20 seconds, because after a full restart the TV takes a while to list its devices. If none is on, Broom opens as usual (or the morning Briefing).

What that means day to day:

| You switch on with… | You land on |
|---|---|
| The Fire TV / set-top box remote (the device wakes the TV) | That device's HDMI input |
| The TV remote, with the device in standby | Broom Home |
| The TV remote, with the device left on | That device's HDMI input |

It also covers a common TV quirk: the device sends "switch to me" while the TV is still starting up, the TV misses it and stays on its home screen.

**To make it work:**
1. **On the TV:** turn HDMI-CEC on. It has a different name on each brand: *HDMI CEC* or *HDMI control* (Xiaomi, stock Android TV), *T-Link* (TCL), *BRAVIA Sync* (Sony), *EasyLink* (Philips). It's usually under Settings → Inputs or Device Preferences. Turn on "auto power on" / "auto device off" too if there are such options. Claude can also turn it on over ADB (see `CLAUDE.md`).
2. **On the device:** turn CEC on there as well. On a Fire TV: Settings → Display & Sounds → **HDMI CEC Device Control**.
3. Check: the device's name should appear on its HDMI tile in Broom's Inputs row.

**Don't want it?** Quick settings → **Power-on** switches between *Broom (or HDMI device that's on)* and *Last input*.

**Volume from a Fire TV remote** isn't sent over CEC to TV speakers. Set it up on the Fire TV instead: Settings → Equipment Control → Manage Equipment → add your TV. It needs a genuine Alexa Voice Remote with volume buttons; some third-party replacement remotes look identical but aren't accepted.

## Pop-ups (over any app or HDMI input)

- **Cricket**: when an India / IPL match goes live, each wicket, and every 50 runs
- **Football**: goals, kick-off and full time for big clubs
- **Markets**: your watchlist alerts
- **Sleep timer** warning

Each card shows for about 10 seconds in the bottom-right corner, then fades.

## Network monitor and TV stats

- **Network monitor** checks the connection every 2 minutes and shows:
  - latency chart, outages and 24 h uptime
  - Wi-Fi signal and link speed
  - a speed test every 3 hours when the TV is idle, or **Run speed test now**
- **TV stats & savings**: memory and storage now, the biggest memory users, and all packages. The "before" numbers come from the Xiaomi Mi TV this was built on, not from your TV, unless Claude has rebuilt the page for your TV (see [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md#on-non-xiaomi-tvs-whats-the-same-and-what-needs-extra-steps)).

## If something goes wrong

- **Home doesn't load:** Broom shows a plain "safe mode" app list instead of a black screen.
- **The Home button opens the old home screen again** (usually after a system update): run the installer again from your computer.
- **Weather says "Finding your location…"** for more than a minute: check Settings → Device Preferences → Location is on, or use Weather → **Change area** and pick your area from the list.
- **Switching on from the Fire TV / set-top box remote stays on Broom:** HDMI-CEC is off on the TV or on the device. See [HDMI devices and power-on](#hdmi-devices-and-power-on-hdmi-cec).
