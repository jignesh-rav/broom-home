# Packages that are safe to DISABLE. The installer uses this list on every TV (step 2 of setup).
# Written for Xiaomi Mi TVs (PatchWall, Android TV 9); the Google ones apply to any Android TV.
# Brand extras live in debloat-<brand>.txt (for example debloat-tcl.txt) and are added automatically.
# Packages missing on your TV are skipped. Disabled = switched off, not deleted. `undo` turns every one back on.
#
# Sections: lines under a "# --- optional" heading are asked about separately (default: keep).
# Delete or comment out (#) any line you never want switched off.
#
# Never listed (remote, HDMI, voice, casting, Play Store, updates must keep working):
#   com.droidlogic.*, com.android.bluetooth, com.google.android.tv.remote.service, com.android.tv,
#   com.google.android.katniss, com.google.android.gms, com.android.vending, com.google.android.apps.mediashell,
#   com.xiaomi.mitv.updateservice, com.mitv.tvhome.atv (PatchWall: it owns the input-source pop-up)
